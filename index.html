Who sits behind the yellow arrow?

A familiar mark can sit above very different company details. Before treating two names as neighbours, read the operator information attached to the exact domain.

  • Operator line — write the legal company exactly as it appears in the footer or terms page.
  • Licence line — copy the regulator and reference number instead of relying on a shared brand name.
  • Support route — keep the help page, email route and complaint path with the same domain.
  • Registration route — note whether the page opens a new account form or a returning-player login.

Those four notes turn a familiar logo into something you can actually compare. If one line is missing, leave a question beside the destination and check it before continuing.
